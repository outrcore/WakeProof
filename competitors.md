# Competitors

Snapshot: July 2026. App Store ratings, pricing, and features change frequently; refresh this document at least quarterly.

## Market categories

WakeProof competes across two overlapping categories:

1. **Mission alarms** that require puzzles, movement, barcode scans, or photos.
2. **Smart wake/sleep apps** that estimate sleep stages and wake inside a time window.

The first category validates the anti-snooze problem. The second validates demand for gentler and more personalized waking. Neither category should dictate MVP scope.

## Mission-alarm competitors

| Product | Observed strengths | Strategic weakness/opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Alarmy | Large incumbent; math, memory, movement, photo, barcode, typing, walking, object recognition, wake checks, mission chaining, AlarmKit adoption | Breadth is not differentiating; user trust can break when matching is too permissive or too strict |
| SuperAlarm | Broad mission bundle, wake checks, Face ID/object scanning, Watch support | Confirms bundled challenges are table stakes |
| Nuj | Barcode commitment, lock windows, optional financial consequences | Strong behavioral wedge; smaller feature breadth; financial stakes add support and policy complexity |
| Captain Wake | Polished new entrant with photo/object and cognitive missions | Demonstrates low barrier to shipping a competent clone |
| Awaken / Rise / Wakey / Solve To Snooze | Typing, CAPTCHA, math, memory, steps, and combinations | Grammar/typing variants are content, not moat |
| SnapWake and similar entrants | Social challenges and wake races | Social alone is easy to copy and can distract from reliability |

## Smart-wake and sleep competitors

| Product | Observed strengths | Strategic implication |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep Cycle | Mature smart wake window, sound/motion analysis, Apple Watch haptics | Owns broad sleep-cycle awareness and brand recognition |
| Pillow | Apple Watch/iPhone sleep analysis, heart-rate and movement-based smart wake, large rating base | Competing head-on requires full sleep product credibility |
| AutoSleep / AutoWake ecosystem | Established Apple Watch sleep tracking, privacy, one-time purchase positioning | Shows a paid non-subscription segment exists |
| FlexiAlarm | Watch-focused wake window, haptic and sensitivity controls | Smart wake can be a focused Watch utility |
| Smart Alarm Clock for Watch | Motion and heart-rate claims, independent Watch operation | Confirms long-standing demand but also fragmented reliability |
| New entrants such as WakeUp and SleepMinder | On-device insights, smart wake, Apple Health integration, aggressive feature claims | Low barrier to marketing “AI sleep” and “optimal wake”; claims need scrutiny |

## What is already table stakes

- Math
- Memory
- Typing
- Barcode/QR
- Steps or movement
- Photo/object recognition
- Multiple missions
- Wake checks
- Loud sounds
- Basic streaks and statistics
- Apple Watch haptics in sleep-focused products
- A 10–45 minute smart wake window

## White space

### 1. End-to-end proof of wakefulness

Competitors often optimize one dismissal action. WakeProof should optimize the complete sequence through a delayed check.

### 2. Stronger but humane photo proof

The product opportunity is not merely “AI object recognition.” It is a calibrated live-proof flow with:

- multi-view registration;
- multi-frame attempt capture;
- randomized liveness;
- clear honest-failure guidance;
- accessible fallback;
- measured false accepts and false rejects.

### 3. Reliability as visible product surface

Reliability Center, test alarm, local/system reconciliation, and incident diagnostics can become a trust differentiator.

### 4. Commitment before the alarm

A lock window addresses the user sabotaging tomorrow’s alarm before it rings.

### 5. Caught-cheating UGC

The entertaining shareable moment is the app rejecting a shortcut and then catching the user returning to bed.

## Competitive response assumptions

Assume successful features can be copied in weeks. Defensibility must therefore depend on:

- better calibration;
- stronger reliability data;
- a larger adversarial corpus;
- superior onboarding and recovery;
- brand and creator distribution;
- faster iteration;
- user trust.

## Competitor research checklist

For every quarterly refresh:

- App Store rating count and recent-rating trend
- Pricing and trial structure
- AlarmKit adoption
- Mission list
- Snooze behavior
- Wake-check behavior
- Photo-spoof behavior
- Emergency escape
- Watch support
- Health-data claims
- Privacy disclosures
- Top positive and negative review themes
- Recent release cadence
- UGC and paid creative patterns
